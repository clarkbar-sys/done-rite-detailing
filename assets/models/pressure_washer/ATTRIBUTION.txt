homemade_flamethrower.glb is the Sketchfab download, unmodified — the file name is
the artist's and the game's use of it is not. It is what DetailingTool.Id.POWER_WASH
puts in the player's hands; ToolModel fits it into that tool's catalogue extent and
nothing here is re-scaled, re-oriented or re-textured on the way in. The three .png
files beside it are the textures Godot extracts out of the .glb on import, which is
why they are committed alongside their .import sidecars.
