tire_cleaner.glb and window_cleaner.glb are processed derivatives of "Kitchen Spray"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kitchen-spray-2a5debf2fd58487e91fce90487c22e26) by Jesus Osco (https://sketchfab.com/gsus.vfx),
licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
See scripts/build-tire-cleaner.py for what the processing does, and
scripts/build-window-cleaner.py for the recolour that makes the second
one out of the first.

Every other model in this folder is the project's own.
