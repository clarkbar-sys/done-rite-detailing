# Tutorial narration

One section per take — see issue #221 for where the take names come from.
Headings below are the take names exactly (lowercase, `full_loop` with an
underscore), so `scripts/narrate.sh` can turn each section straight into
`build/narration/<take-name>.wav` and the capture harness can pair footage
and voiceover by name alone.

Keep every section to one or two short sentences. The acceptance bar (issue
#175) is a player who understands wash → cleaner → buff without reading
paragraphs, not a transcript of the how-to-play screen — that screen already
exists and says more than this needs to.

## spray

Grab the power washer, aim it at the paint, and hold. That's the wash pass — it only lifts mud.

## rinse

Keep the water moving until the panel's bare paint again, no mud left behind.

## sponge

Bare paint takes product, and every surface wants its own bottle: sponge for the body, cleaner for glass, tyre cleaner for wheels.

## rag

Wipe the product off with the drying rag. That's the shine, and the only way a panel counts as finished.

## full_loop

Wash off the mud, lay down the right product for the surface, then buff it to a shine. Same three steps, every panel.
